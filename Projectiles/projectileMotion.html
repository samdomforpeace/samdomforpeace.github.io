Objectives

Students will:

(1.) Discuss projectiles.
(2.) Discuss each case of a projectile motion.
(3.) Derive the formulas for several parameters for each case of a projectile motion.
(4.) Solve applied problems on projectiles.

  • Symbols and Meanings

  • To solve for a specified variable for each formula, please review Solved Examples - Literal Equations
  • $U$ = initial velocity
  • $U_x$ = horizontal component of the initial velocity
  • $U_y$ = vertical component of the initial velocity
  • $V$ = final velocity
  • $\theta$ = angle of projection with the horizontal
  • $a$ = acceleration
  • $g$ = acceleration due to gravity
  • $t$ = time of the motion of the projectile
  • $h$ = height of the projectile at time, $t$
  • $T$ = time of flight of the projectile
  • $H$ = maximum height
  • $t_H$ = time to reach the maximum height
  • $R$ = range
  • $R_{max}$ = maximum range
  • $\phi$ = angle of elevation/depression
  • $h_{below}$ = height below the horizontal
