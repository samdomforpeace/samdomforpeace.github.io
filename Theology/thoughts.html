(1.) The LORD thy GOD alone is Holy Father. Only Him. There is no other.
Stop addressing the Pope as holy father.
Stop worshipping/praising your Bishops, Priests, Apostles, Prophets, Pastors, Evangelists, Ministers or any human being with any title/function.
All of them are just ordinary people.

(2.) The Bible includes the words of GOD.
However, it is not the WORD of GOD, neither is it the word(s) of GOD.
It is a compilation of writings by mankind (men and women).

(3.) JESUS CHRIST is not the son of David.
You can call Him a descendant of David. But do not call Him the son of David.
And there is definitely no need to call Him the son of David if you want any favor from Him.

If you want any favor from JESUS CHRIST, call Him any of the Names found in the Praise and Worship section.

Calling Him appropriate Names does not guarantee that He will grant your request.
GOD does what He wills.

The fact that GOD (JESUS CHRIST) chose to be very humble to be born a human should never make us neglect the fact that He is GOD.
Most importantly, He was not born by human means even though He was human.
Joseph and Mary did not have any intercourse for JESUS CHRIST to be born.
JESUS CHRIST was born by the HOLY SPIRIT. Let that sink in.
In all applicable verses in the Bible, He never referred to Joseph as His father.
