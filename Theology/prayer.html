Our FATHER in Heaven

Beloved,

Grace and peace to you from GOD our FATHER and the LORD JESUS CHRIST...$\boldsymbol{Philippians\:\:1:2}$

If there is one prayer that you should pray/sing every day and every hour, it is the LORD's prayer (Our FATHER in Heaven prayer)
It is the most powerful prayer. A pure heart, a clean mind, and a clear conscience is necessary for it. - Samuel Dominic Chukwuemeka

Let us medidate on the LORD's prayer.
As I wrote in one of my quotes, the requirements are:
(1.) A pure heart
(2.) A clean mind
(3.) A clear conscience

These include:
Belief in GOD.
Faith in GOD.
No grudges against anyone.
No thoughts of evil.
No thoughts of revenge.
No thoughts of anger.
No bad wish.

Rather than have bad thoughts against someone, commit the person to GOD. Let GOD handle it. Move on. - Samuel Chukwuemeka

$\boldsymbol{Matthew\:\:6:10,\;\;Luke\;\;11:2}$
Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven
Faith, Reverence and Total Submission
Acknowledge the LORD GOD as our Judge (Judiciary), our lawgiver (Legislature) and our King (Executive): Isaiah 33:23
Talk to GOD about His plans for us...plans to prosper us and not to harm us; plans to give us a future and a hope: Jeremiah 29:11
Thank GOD for His will for us and pray to Him that we accomplish His will for us: 1 Thessalonians 5:18
Talk to GOD that we are calling upon Him, and we are praying to Him, and we are seeking Him, and we believe in His promises that He will listen to us and answer us: Jeremiah 29:12 - 13, Jeremiah 33:3, Matthew 7:7 - 8


$\boldsymbol{Matthew\:\:6:11,\;\;Luke\;\;11:3}$

Thanksgiving and Requests
Thank GOD for life. Thank GOD for being able to talk to Him. Thank GOD for the bread He gave us yesterday and past times: 1 Thessalonians 5:18

Talk to GOD that He knows all our needs including the ones we know and the ones we do not know...Matthew 6:32
Talk to GOD that we seek Him, and we need Him, and we need His Righteousness, and we believe that He will grant us all our needs...Matthew 6:33, Matthew 7:11, Romans 8:32
Mention only the good needs: your needs; petitions; requests; needs of your family, loved ones, friends, church, society, community, etc.
